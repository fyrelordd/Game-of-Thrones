The Trials Begin

1. When faced with a decision that shapes the future, how dost thou approach the challenge?

2. In the vaults of kings and the halls of legends, two treasures gleam: Honor and Gold. Which dost thou hold above all?

3. When the drums of war echo through the mountains, what do you seek most in the ranks at your side?

4. The realms are vast and the winds restless. By which path dost thou choose to journey?

5. When betrayal cuts deep and wrongs are laid bare, how dost thou answer the call of justice?

6. When foes stand before thee, how dost thou choose to end their tale?

7. When the fires of ambition burn within thee, which path dost thou walk?

8. When the drums of war sound and the battlefield calls, how dost thou prefer to fight?
