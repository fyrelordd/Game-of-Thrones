About This Realm

In a land where banners fly and steel decides fate, each soul walks a path toward allegiance. This is no mere game — it is a reckoning of character, a measure of honor, cunning, bravery, and loyalty.

Through a series of questions, the realm peers into your heart. Your choices summon truths — do you seek power or peace? Will you command fleets upon the sea, or defend your stronghold in the hills? Are your words as deadly as your blade?

At journey’s end, the realm shall name your House — not by blood, but by bond. Stark or Targaryen, Greyjoy or Tyrell, each House holds virtues that echo within you.

Welcome to your reckoning.
